description: "A keyboard-driven, Bloomberg-style terminal for exploring the scientific literature. Find papers by title, DOI or plain-language idea (keyword and semantic search over OpenAlex), then grow an interactive citation graph by expanding each paper's references and citations. Several starting papers can share one auto-laid-out canvas, each paper gets a one-line AI summary from Claude, and you can annotate or hide nodes. Optional accounts with role-based access let signed-in users save, autosave and resume research sessions."
